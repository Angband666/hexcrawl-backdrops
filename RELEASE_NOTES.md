# Hexcrawl Backdrops 0.3.0

## New in 0.3.0
- **Show a picture to the players without activating the scene.** The button **Show to players** in the variant window (also `/backdrop show` and a macro) shares the chosen picture with all players as a large image window. Your own view, for example the overland map, stays as it is. Handy for showing the landscape hex by hex; activate the scene only when you need tokens on it.
- **Search field** in the variant window: type part of a name or a hex number, e.g. `0412`.
- **Clear state:** the window says whether the scene is active (players see every change at once) or not (the change is saved for later).
- **Activate and View buttons**, and a double-click on a picture chooses it and activates the scene in one step.
- **A Hexcrawl Backdrops button of its own in the scene controls on the left.** It opens the variant window and has a tool to show the picture to the players (can be switched off in the module settings).
- **Fixed:** the arrow keys in the variant window no longer pan the canvas behind it.

Thanks again to the tester whose notes led to these changes. Update in Foundry as usual; your imported scenes keep working.

*Scenes for the Dolmenwood RPG.* Free, non-commercial. Requires Foundry VTT v14 and the Dolmenwood core books.

**159 encounter places, 200 hex landscapes and 20 fairy road and realm scenes**, each with variants for night, dusk, rain and mist, thunderstorm, seasons and campsites. Scene names in English or German. About 870 MB.

## Install
In Foundry: Add-on Modules, Install Module, manifest URL:

    https://github.com/Angband666/hexcrawl-backdrops/releases/latest/download/module.json

## Why this exists
I made these scenes for my own Dolmenwood campaign and share them for free in case other game masters find them useful. Some of the AI images are not perfect. If one does not work for you, just pick a different one. This is about a good selection of backdrops, not about perfection. This is my first release on GitHub, so please be patient with me. I simply hope it will be useful for other game masters.

## AI disclosure
**All images are AI-generated.** I made every one locally with FLUX.2 klein 4B (Apache 2.0) from my own scene descriptions and checked them myself. No official Dolmenwood art, maps or text were used as input or reference. Code: MIT. Images: CC BY-NC 4.0.
